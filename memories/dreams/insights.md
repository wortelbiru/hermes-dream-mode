## Interpreting Session Gaps

- Long gaps with incomplete tasks usually indicate the user stepped away, not assistant failure.
- Do not flag idle time as a quality issue unless evidence shows the assistant stalled.

## Quality Principles

- Evidence over assumptions: cite specific session moments, not vague impressions.
- Honest self-critique: acknowledge mistakes plainly, no defensive framing.
- Context efficiency: prefer snippets and bookends over full session reads.
- Durable learning: only record lessons that generalize beyond one session.
